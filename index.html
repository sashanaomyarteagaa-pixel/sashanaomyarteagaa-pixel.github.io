Fuck My Birthday is Coming up

This project examines the emotional complexities of birthday celebrations in the face of mental health challenges.

Through a thought provoking game, players choose whether to celebrate or forgo their birthday, leading to a series of scenarios that reflect the internal battles of the character.

The goal is to encourage players to confront anxieties and explore the significance of changing inner dialogue.
